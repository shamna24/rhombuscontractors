QUALITY ASSURANCE & NDT RIGOR

Testing Protocols Backing Every Joint.

Certification without relentless verification is meaningless. Rhombus Contractors deploys certified Level II Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) technicians and advanced 3D scanning to ensure 100% compliance.

01

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Ultrasonic (UT), Magnetic Particle (MPI), and Liquid Penetrant testing conducted by certified technicians on all full-penetration structural joints.

02

EN 10204 3.1 Mill Certs

100% heat-number traceability on incoming structural steel plates and profiles directly from primary mills, validating chemical and yield strength.

03

3D Laser Dimensional Audits

Pre-dispatch laser measurement comparing physical assemblies against Tekla BIM models, ensuring tolerances stay strictly within ±0.5mm.

04

Coating Adhesion & DFT

Cross-hatch adhesion verification, dry film thickness (DFT) magnetic testing, and 3,000-hour salt-spray chamber exposure for corrosion durability.
