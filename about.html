OUR ORIGIN & JOURNEY

From Heavy Metallurgy to Modern Skylines.

Rhombus Contractors was established with a singular conviction: to bridge metallurgical mastery with modern architectural vision. What began as a dedicated fabrication shop for industrial framing has matured into a premier multi-disciplinary contractor delivering turnkey steel framing, architectural metalwork, and precision coatings.

Today, our certified engineers and master fabricators collaborate directly with architects, general contractors, and structural consultants. By keeping steel supply, robotic fabrication, automated finishing, and site erection under one accountable roof, we eliminate subcontractor friction and protect client schedules.

2008

Metallurgy & Heavy Fabrication Founding

Founded with a core focus on heavy structural steel beams, certified welding, and high-yield rebar distribution for industrial facilities.

2014

45,000 Sq Ft Plant & CNC Automation

Commissioned a high-capacity facility featuring 5-axis CNC plasma cutting, automated beam coping lines, and dedicated robotic welding bays.

2019

Digital Engineering & BIM LOD 400

Pioneered integrated Tekla and Revit digital twin workflows, enabling zero-clash fabrication detailing and computerized file transfers directly to shop machinery.

2023 – Present

Sustainable Turnkey Structural Contracting

Expanded into end-to-end subcontracting, environmentally certified Qualicoat architectural powder coating, and complex facade engineering across landmark developments.

Parametric BIM & CNC Profiling

Every beam, connection gusset, and anchor bolt is detailed in 3D LOD 400 before a single torch touches metal—eliminating field modification delays.
